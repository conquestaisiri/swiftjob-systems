# Trust and credibility review

The strongest trust repairs are ownership-bound candidate access, explicit reference-bound assessment links, private candidate responses, required resume validation, honest checker copy, no silent third-party requests, explicit social-link availability, and server-side logout revocation.

The site still needs a live designated mailbox receipt, confirmed sender-domain authentication, and a final content review of employer-facing claims before a public readiness decision. Those are evidence gaps, not inferred successes.
